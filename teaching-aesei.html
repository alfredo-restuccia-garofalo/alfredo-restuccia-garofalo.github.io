BIM Training for Engineers and Technical Professionals

For approximately four years, Alfredo Restuccia Garofalo has carried out BIM-related teaching and professional training activities for engineers and technical professionals as a BIM expert/trainer in collaboration with A.E.S.E.I. S.r.l., spin-off of the Polytechnic University of Bari.

A.E.S.E.I. S.r.l. is associated with the name Architectural & Engineering Survey of Environmental and Infrastructure. The training activity focuses on the practical use of BIM-oriented workflows for structural and infrastructural applications.

Training topics

  • BIM methodology and information management
  • Structural BIM and interoperability with FEM software
  • Scan-to-BIM/HBIM workflows and 3D survey integration
  • Parametric modelling and digital workflows for engineers
  • Use of BIM for existing buildings and infrastructures

Professional orientation

  • Practical training for engineers and technicians
  • Application-oriented BIM workflows
  • Digital modelling for structural assessment
  • Integration between modelling, analysis and documentation
  • Support to professional upskilling in digital construction
